5 Rules · Read them.

01

Gameplay Comes First

If I suggest a game or give instructions, please listen so we can actually play. Arguing, ignoring instructions, or trying to take over the lobby ruins it for everyone. Ignoring instructions tells me you're here just for yourself.

02

Give Me Space

Please don't crowd me or get right in my face in-game. Back up a few steps so I can see, hear, and play properly. Being close to me might make you think I'll pay attention to the closest person — when really it makes me ignore them.

03

Keep the Noise Under Control

No shouting over each other. One person talking at a time works best. Talking over others guarantees nobody hears you properly. Including me.

04

Questions, Photos & High-Fives

These happen AFTER I do any minigames or videos — not in the middle of them.

05

Respect = More Lobbies

If a lobby stays respectful and fun, I'll keep doing them. It's that straightforward.

Most of these are the same as pepsi dee's — his rules are very good and I tweaked some. I did not think of most of these from the top of my head. Credit where it's due.
