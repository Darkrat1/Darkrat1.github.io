🦍 DarkRat1 — Fan Code

Lobby
Rules

Keep it fun, keep it respectful. Follow these and we'll run lobbies all day long.

01
Gameplay Comes First
If I suggest a game or give instructions, please listen so we can actually play. Arguing, ignoring instructions, or trying to take over the lobby ruins it for everyone. Ignoring instructions tells me you're here for just yourself.
02
Give Me Space
Please don't crowd me or get right in my face in-game. Back up a few steps so I can see, hear, and play properly. Being close to me might make you think I'll pay attention to the closest person — when really it makes me ignore them.
03
Keep the Noise Under Control
No shouting over each other. One person talking at a time works best. Talking over others guarantees nobody hears you properly. Including me.
04
Questions, Photos & High-Fives
These happen after I do any minigames or videos — not in the middle.
05
Respect = More Lobbies
If a lobby stays respectful and fun, I'll keep doing them. Simple as that.
Credits: Most of these rules are the same as Pepsi Dee's — his are very good and I tweaked some. I did not think of most of these from the top of my head. Credit where it's due.
